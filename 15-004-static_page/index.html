CDs and Vinyl

My parents loved music and we had a full stash of CDs and vinyl, so I guess it's no surprise that music has captivated me since my earliest memories. From my first guitar or the very first CD I purchased with my own pocket money, to 25+ years later and I'm still on the prowl for musical "memorabilia" or just new track or artist to sink my teeth into. I'm here for it all!

Collecting CDs

The first CD I was ever gifted was Backstreet Boys' Backstreet's Back. I'd study all there was to study in the little CD booklet, and learn every lyric. Soon thereafter I purchased my very own CD, with my own money. Backstreet Boys' Millenium.

Fast forward several years and musical experimentation later, and it's a weekday at home after school. I was in my room listening to my copy of Nickelback's Silver Side Up when, at some point, I heard noise and screams blaring from my sister's room next door. I soon realised she was listening to something I'd never heard before.

What follows is the sequence of events as I recall them.

  1. Turn down my music
  2. Eavesdrop for approximately 1 minute
  3. Uncontrollable urge to know more
  4. Knock on sister's door
  5. Me: "What you listening to, who is this?"
  6. Her: "Nirvana"
  7. Me: "I was listening to Nickelback"
  8. Her: "Ok that's cool..."
  9. Me: "Want to swap?"
  10. Her: "Ok"

It was Nirvana's From The Muddy Banks Of The Wishkah. That album and band have remained one of my all time favourites ever since.

Several years ago I lent that CD to a friend and sadly since then it's been lost to the sands of time!

Collecting Vinyl

Apart from the audio quality There's just something about vinyl! There's no quick, skip to the next track, button. From browsing through the interesting album covers, taking out the inner sleeve, checking that the vinyl is clean enough, lifting the dust cover of the vinyl player, placing the album on and the always satisfying drop of the needle, the entire process feels more intentional. With that, the resurgence of vinyl today has brought about new and interesting pressings, coloured and patterned vinyl, which I am a total sucker for!
